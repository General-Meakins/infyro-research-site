AI Disruption Scorecard

A consistent scoring lens applied across the 14-company coverage basket, rating each name on five factors that capture both AI disruption risk and AI-driven upside — rather than a single risk score, since the basket spans software incumbents exposed to automation and infrastructure suppliers who benefit from it.

Each factor is scored 0–4. Two factors measure risk (higher = more exposed); three measure protection or upside (higher = more shielded, or more of a beneficiary). The Net AI Disruption Score averages each side to a common scale, then takes the difference: avg(Pricing exposure, Margin exposure) − avg(Moat durability, Demand tailwind, AI response). Positive = net disruption risk. Negative = net AI beneficiary. Range runs roughly −4 to +4.
